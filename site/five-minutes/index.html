stardelt

Your own data platform.

Open source, on your own Kubernetes cluster. One sign-in, one interface, one API.

stardelt is an open-source data platform. In five minutes: why it exists, what it is, and how you can take part.

Data platforms are usually rented.

Your tables, your code and your people live in someone else's product, under someone else's terms and prices. Leaving becomes a project of its own.

The problem we start from. It is not about cloud or not cloud; it is about who controls the platform your data work depends on.

What stardelt is

  • Proven open-source parts, installed and connected for you
  • Query, load, schedule and show data in one place
  • Runs on the Kubernetes cluster you already have
Nova is the one interface: catalogs, recent loads and queries, and the services. Under it are Trino, Iceberg, Airflow, Superset and JupyterHub, as they are upstream.

Why it is different

sovereign

Your data stays with you

Everything runs in your cluster. No required outbound calls, no license server.

open

You can leave

Tables are open Iceberg and Parquet files on storage you choose.

permissive

No license surprises

Permissive licenses throughout, with one documented exception.

These three are the promise. Each one is documented on the website under Architecture.

A team's day

Load

Files, APIs and databases into tables

Query

SQL in the browser, a notebook or a script

Schedule

SQL and notebooks, pipelines from Git

Show

Dashboards, and who sees what by group

This is the path the product is built around. If there is time, show it live in Nova.
